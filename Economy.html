Economy

What is Economy?

A quantitative and theoretical social science investigating how societies allocate scarce resources, generate wealth, and respond to economic incentives.

The programme covers microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics, monetary policy, and international trade alongside wizarding currency systems, Gringotts reserve dynamics, and enchanted commodity markets. Students develop advanced analytical and forecasting skills to advise governments, financial institutions, and enterprises on growth, stability, and strategic resource management.
