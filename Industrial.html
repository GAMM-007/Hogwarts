Industrial engineer

What is Industrial engineer?

An engineering discipline dedicated to analysing, designing, and optimising complex operational systems involving people, materials, information, equipment, and energy.

Students study operations research, statistical quality control, supply chain logistics, ergonomics, and systems simulation applied to both Muggle manufacturing and enchanted production networks. Graduates eliminate inefficiencies, reduce operational risk, and engineer streamlined workflows across large-scale organisations.
