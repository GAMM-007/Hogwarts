Medicine

What is Medicine?

An intensive clinical and scientific degree preparing students to diagnose, treat, and prevent human illness and injury across both conventional and magical pathology.

The programme combines foundational biomedical sciences—human anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, microbiology, and pharmacology—with diagnostic spellwork, restorative healing charms, and potion-based therapeutics. Structured around hospital rotations and clinical mentorship, it trains compassionate, disciplined physicians capable of handling routine care and acute emergencies alike.
