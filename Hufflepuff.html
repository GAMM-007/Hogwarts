Hufflepuff House

Founded in 1591 by Helga Hufflepuff, the house was established on the principle of welcoming all who were willing to work hard, show patience, and care for others without prejudice. In this university incarnation of Hogwarts, Hufflepuff has evolved into the Faculty of Medicine and Veterinary Sciences, training future physicians, surgeons, and specialists in both human and magical creature health.

Helga's legendary devotion to fairness, loyalty, and practical skill translates directly into clinical care, where every patient—human or beast—receives the same thorough attention. Students study foundational biomedical modules—including human and comparative anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, and clinical diagnostics—alongside restorative healing charms, potion-based therapeutics, and magical creature care. The house's traditional warmth and camaraderie shape a curriculum centred on patient-centred care, medical ethics, and interdisciplinary teamwork across hospital wards and sanctuaries.

From anatomy laboratories to clinical rotations and creature rehabilitation clinics, Hufflepuff prepares students to combine scientific rigour with steady, compassionate practice.
