Education

What is Education

A comprehensive degree devoted to the theory, psychology, and practice of teaching, curriculum design, and human development.

Students study cognitive learning theory, educational psychology, inclusive pedagogy, assessment methods, and classroom leadership, tailored to both foundational Muggle subjects and progressive magical instruction. Through supervised teaching placements, graduates are trained to mentor diverse learners, foster critical thinking, and cultivate disciplined, ethical scholarship in future generations.
