Physiotherapy

What is Physiotherapy?

A clinical movement science degree focused on restoring mobility, physical strength, and functional independence to patients recovering from injury, surgery, or chronic musculoskeletal and neurological conditions.

The curriculum integrates kinesiology, biomechanics, neuroanatomy, therapeutic exercise, and manual therapy with gentle restorative charms and rehabilitative hydrotherapy. Graduates work closely with patients over sustained recovery timelines, combining anatomical precision with patience and encouragement.
