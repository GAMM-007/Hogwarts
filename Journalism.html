Journalism

What is journalism

A practice-driven degree focused on researching, verifying, and reporting matters of public interest with accuracy, independence, and ethical integrity.

Combining traditional newsroom standards—investigative reporting, media law, data journalism, and editorial writing—with enchanted print, live magical broadcasting, and archival verification, the programme trains reporters to uncover hidden truths, scrutinise institutions, and hold both Muggle and magical authorities accountable.
