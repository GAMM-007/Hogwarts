Law

What is Law?

A rigorous academic and professional degree dedicated to the study of legal systems, statutory interpretation, constitutional frameworks, and the administration of justice across both Muggle and wizarding societies.

Students examine criminal law, contracts, property, civil procedure, and international treaties alongside wizarding statutes, magical creature rights, and Ministry regulatory law. Through intensive moot court advocacy, legal drafting, and ethical training, graduates are prepared to act as barristers, solicitors, judges, and legal counsel capable of defending civil order under pressure.
