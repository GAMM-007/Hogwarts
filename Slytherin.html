Slytherin House

Founded in 989 by Salazar Slytherin, the house was originally established to gather sharp minds defined by ambition, resourcefulness, and a clear-eyed grasp of power. In this university incarnation of Hogwarts, Slytherin serves as the Faculty of Economics and Political Science, shaping future strategists, policy analysts, diplomats, and institutional leaders.

Salazar prized determination and cunning, qualities that translate into reading incentives, anticipating market shifts, and navigating complex political trade-offs. Students study core academic modules—including macroeconomics, econometrics, public policy, comparative politics, and international relations—set alongside wizarding trade regimes, resource governance, and magical statecraft. The house's traditional focus on lineage and influence becomes a rigorous study of institutions, power structures, and strategic decision-making rather than mere prestige.

From debating fiscal policy in the dungeons to modelling crises and negotiations, Slytherin trains students to understand how wealth and authority are built, contested, and sustained.
