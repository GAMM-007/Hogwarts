Gryffindor House

Founded in 1587 by Godric Gryffindor, the house was originally established for wizards of bold spirit and fierce conviction. In this modern university incarnation of Hogwarts, Gryffindor has evolved into the Faculty of Law and Public Justice, training future advocates, legal theorists, and defenders of civil order.

Godric valued courage above all else, and within the legal programme that bravery translates into standing up in court, challenging unjust precedents, and arguing difficult cases under pressure. Students study core legal subjects—such as constitutional law, criminal procedure, contracts, and statutory interpretation—alongside magical jurisprudence and wizarding statutes. The house's traditional emphasis on chivalry and honour shapes its curriculum around professional ethics, due process, and defending those who cannot defend themselves.

From moot court debates in the common room to analysing landmark rulings, Gryffindor prepares students to treat the law not merely as a rulebook, but as a living instrument of justice.
