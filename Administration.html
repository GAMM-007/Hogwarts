Administration

What is Administration?

A management-focused degree designed to train future executives, directors, and organisational strategists in leading complex institutions effectively.

Students master organisational behaviour, corporate finance, operations management, marketing strategy, and human resources, integrated with the governance of magical enterprises, research foundations, and cross-border logistics. Emphasis is placed on decision-making under uncertainty, negotiation, and building resilient, high-performing organisations.
